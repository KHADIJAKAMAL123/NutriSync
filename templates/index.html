About NutriSync

Your high-performance health command center designed to bring precision, transparency, and data privacy to your daily fitness journey.

Core Purpose

NutriSync bridges the gap between raw physiological metrics and daily actionable habits. Built on client-side calculation engines and local storage architecture, NutriSync ensures your personal health metrics remain completely private, fast, and accessible across all devices without requiring cloud registration or data sharing.

Harris-Benedict BMR Algorithms

Calculates precise Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) based on revised Harris-Benedict formulas to establish accurate daily caloric maintenance baselines.

LocalStorage Data Security

All micro-goal logs, calorie entries, and water tracker history are preserved locally in your browser storage. Zero tracking cookies, zero external telemetry, 100% data privacy.

Precision Macro Splits

Automated 30/40/30 protein, carbohydrate, and fat distribution models customized to individual activity factors, helping you balance muscle building and fat loss.

Why NutriSync?

  • All-In-One Dashboard: Track hydration, log meals, compute macronutrients, and view workout routines in one seamless space.
  • Real-Time Analytics: Interactive Chart.js gauges and instant progress visuals give immediate feedback on daily targets.
  • Zero Latency: Immediate, dynamic recalculations on input changes without full page reloads or remote latency.
